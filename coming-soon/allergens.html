Allergen Information

Last updated: 2 November 2026

How Morning Box uses your allergy and dietary information, and what filtering can and cannot do.

How we keep you safe

Every ingredient in Morning Box has an allergen record supplied and confirmed by our Partner Bakeries. When you mark an allergy or dietary requirement (and agree to us using it), we never recommend, swap in or add anything that contains it.

Breakfasts for customers with allergies are prepared only by Partner Bakeries approved to handle them, and are labelled separately.

Allergens we track

Our records cover gluten (wheat), milk, egg, peanut, tree nuts, sesame and soy. If you have an allergy we don’t list, add it under “Other allergies” and our team checks it with the bakery for every morning you order.

Traces and cross-contact

Our Partner Bakeries handle common allergens in the same kitchen. Filtering cannot rule out cross-contact and is not a guarantee that any breakfast is allergen-free. If you have a severe allergy, contact Help & Support before ordering so we can check with the bakery.

If you have a reaction

In an emergency call 998 for an ambulance (or 999) or seek medical help first. Then report it from the order screen (Allergy concern) so we can investigate with the bakery the same day.
