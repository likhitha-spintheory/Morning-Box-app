Cookie & Device Storage Notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Morning Box uses only essential device storage by default; analytics run only after you say yes.

What this covers

Cookies, local storage, session storage and similar identifiers on the Morning Box website and app (“device storage”). It works alongside our Privacy Policy.

We do not use device storage to build advertising profiles from allergy or dietary data.

Your choices

On first visit we show a banner with Accept all, Reject non-essential and Settings, each equally easy to choose. You can change your choice any time in Profile › Privacy & data › Cookie settings, or with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of the home page. Blocking essential storage in your browser may stop sign-in or plan saving from working.

Changes and contact

We update this Notice when we add or remove tools. Questions: info@morningbox.ae or Help & Support.
