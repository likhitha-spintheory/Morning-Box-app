Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Morning Box uses your information to take, prepare and deliver your breakfast orders, and to keep your allergy and preference settings so suggestions fit you. We never sell your data, and we ask for your explicit consent before using allergy or other health-related information.

1. Who we are

Morning Box is operated by Studio Nest L.L.C-FZ, Meydan Grandstand, 6th Floor, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (“Morning Box”, “we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data we collect to run the platform, under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the “PDPL”).

Partner Bakeries, Delivery Partners and our payment provider receive limited data to fulfil your order. For data they use for their own legal duties (for example, food-safety or payment records), they act as separate controllers under their own privacy notices.

Privacy contact: info@morningbox.ae or Help & Support in the app.

3. Allergy and dietary information

Allergy and dietary details can reveal information about your health, which is sensitive personal data under the PDPL. We therefore:

  • ask for your separate, explicit consent before you save them (an unticked box, not bundled with these terms);
  • use them only to filter suggestions and pass the relevant details for a specific order to the bakery preparing it;
  • never use them for advertising or share them for anyone’s marketing;
  • limit staff access to those who need it for support or food-safety cases.

You can use Morning Box without saving allergy data, but then we cannot filter for you. You can withdraw consent any time in your Morning Profile; this deletes the saved details going forward. Filtering is not a guarantee that food is allergen-free (see our Terms of Service, section 9).

5. Transfers outside the UAE

Some providers, such as Stripe, Resend, Meta and Google, may store or access data outside the UAE, including in the European Union and the United States. We transfer data only to countries with adequate protection recognised under the PDPL, or with safeguards such as contractual protection, or where another lawful condition applies. Sensitive allergy data is kept on Morning Box’s own server and is shared only with the bakery preparing your order.

6. Your rights

Under the PDPL you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold, why, who receives it and how long we keep it, and give you a copy;
  • give you your data in a structured, machine-readable format and transfer it to another provider where technically feasible;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data when it is no longer needed, you withdraw consent, or it was processed unlawfully;
  • restrict or stop processing in certain cases, including objecting to processing for direct marketing;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects (our breakfast suggestions are not such decisions);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

How: use Profile › Privacy & data in the app (download your data, correct your details, turn allergy filtering or offers off, change cookie settings, or delete your account), or contact info@morningbox.ae. We may verify your identity. We reply within 30 days and do not charge for reasonable requests. Some records (for example tax and transaction records) must be kept by law even after you close your account; we will tell you if so.

If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

7. Marketing

We send promotional messages only if you opt in. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can change preferences in the app at any time. Order updates and service messages are not marketing and are always sent.

8. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit (TLS), restricted server access, role-based access, one-time-code sign-in, sessions stored only as secure hashes, logging and staff confidentiality obligations. If a personal data breach is likely to put your privacy or rights at risk, we notify the UAE Data Office and, where required, you, without undue delay. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Cookies and device storage

We use essential storage to keep you signed in and save unfinished plans, and optional analytics only with your consent. See our Cookie & Device Storage Notice for details.

10. Children

Morning Box is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Do not enter allergy information about a child in your own Morning Profile.

11. Changes to this Policy

We update this Policy when our service or the law changes, and show the date at the top. We tell you in the app or by email before material changes take effect, and ask again for consent where required.
